Why this helps

The crib monitor guesses from stillness alone, so it both invents sleep (she was lying quietly awake) and misses it (she slept somewhere it cannot see). Nothing on the Pi can tell the difference — only you can. Each verdict is stored against the reason that block ended, so once there are enough of them the tuning page can say which setting is causing the errors instead of guessing.
